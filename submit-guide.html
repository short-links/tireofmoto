How to Submit Your Site for Indexing (Free Methods)

Step 1: Google Search Console (FREE - Critical)

  1. Go to Google Search Console
  2. Click "Add Property" and enter tireofmoto.com
  3. Verify ownership by adding the HTML meta tag or DNS TXT record
  4. Submit your sitemap at https://tireofmoto.com/sitemap.xml

Step 2: Bing Webmaster Tools (FREE)

  1. Go to Bing Webmaster Tools
  2. Add your domain and verify ownership
  3. Submit sitemap.xml
  4. Use "Url Inspector" for manual indexing of new posts

Step 3: Free Directory Submissions (FREE)

  • 10Best - Travel directory
  • MotoForum.net directories
  • RideApart.com resource pages

Step 4: Free Social Media Posting

Share each new article on: Reddit (r/motorcycles, r/superbike, r/advrider), Facebook Groups, Pinterest, Quora, and YouTube descriptions.
